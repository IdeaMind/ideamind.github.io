IDEA MIND

What We Are

Idea Mind is an applied R&D laboratory. We design embedded hardware at the circuit level, write the firmware that runs on it, and integrate sensors into field-ready devices.

A core group of engineers runs the lab. Consultants from academia and industry join when a project demands specialized capability. When a design is production-ready and you need manufacturing, we connect you to partners we have shipped with before.

We work in underwater communications, unattended ground sensor networks, tactical wearables, AI-integrated performance dashboards, field power systems, and robotic diagnostics. These domains share a common requirement: hardware that works the first time it is powered on in the field.

How We Work

You contact us with a hardware problem. We sit down and discuss it. Then we gather the information needed to validate what is actually needed.

Once the problem is understood, we build the perfect team. Our full network is in play: Academia, Government partnerships, Industry consultants, and Manufacturing experts. A breakthrough shipped by the right team beats having full control of an average project.

With the team in place, we work with you on how the engagement runs. We can manage the project end-to-end or play a specific role inside a larger effort.
